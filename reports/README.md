# Consulting Reports

This folder contains the final consulting report and a separate French executive summary for the EU Merger Control and M&A Regulatory Intelligence case study.

The reports bring together historical European merger-control evidence, statistical analysis and practical recommendations for regulatory due diligence.

The full report examines how historical merger-control information can support early M&A decision-making.

It covers:

- Historical regulatory outcomes and review pathways.
- Phase I and Phase II review durations.
- Sector-level conditional-clearance patterns.
- Statistical testing and adjusted logistic regression.
- Decision-Changing Evidence Score (DCES) prioritisation.
- A proposed 90-day regulatory diligence roadmap.
- Management decision gates, risks and limitations.

The recommendations focus on what an acquiring company should investigate before committing to a transaction.

### Synthèse exécutive — Français

Cette synthèse présente les principaux résultats de l'analyse, leurs implications pour les opérations de fusion-acquisition et les actions recommandées pour préparer une évaluation des risques réglementaires.

Elle s'adresse aux professionnels du conseil, de la stratégie et du M&A.

## Key conclusion

Historical merger-control evidence can help businesses identify areas requiring closer investigation. However, regulatory decisions depend on the specific circumstances of each acquisition.

The recommended approach is to combine historical evidence with transaction-specific competition assessments, legal review and clear management decision points.

## Important note

This is an independent portfolio case study based on public regulatory information and a simulated client scenario.

The recommendations are proposals, not implemented client actions. No transaction-specific legal opinion, regulatory approval or realised commercial benefit is claimed.
