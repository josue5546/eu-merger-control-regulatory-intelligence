# Management Presentation

This folder contains the PowerPoint presentation summarising the EU Merger Control and M&A Regulatory Intelligence analysis.

## Purpose

The presentation translates the main analytical findings into practical information for business leaders considering an acquisition in the European Union.

It focuses on the regulatory questions that could influence transaction planning, timing and execution.

## Presentation overview

The 17-slide presentation covers:

- The business problem and executive recommendation.
- Historical merger activity and regulatory outcomes.
- Phase II initiation and review-duration evidence.
- Differences in conditional-clearance patterns across sectors.
- Adjusted statistical associations and their limitations.
- DCES-based prioritisation of due-diligence activities.
- A proposed 90-day regulatory diligence roadmap.
- Decision gates, risks, next steps and final conclusions.

## Key management message

**Historical evidence should guide the questions, not determine the deal decision.**

The recommended approach is to establish filing obligations, assess competitive overlaps and prepare review-timing contingencies before committing to transaction assumptions.

## Scope

The presentation reflects a simulated advisory engagement. All proposed workstreams and management actions are recommendations rather than implemented activities.
